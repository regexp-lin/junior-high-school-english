# Unit 1 Great people - 词汇背诵检测

建议用时：35 分钟。请先独立完成，再查看文末答案。

## 一、根据中文或首字母提示，写出恰当的单词（每题 1 分）

1. Tu Youyou is an __________ (杰出的) scientist.
2. Some young people prefer __________ (老式的) music to modern pop music.
3. Shakespeare wrote a literary __________ (经典作品).
4. It is an __________ (普通的) school day.
5. Li Bai was a great __________ (诗人) in Chinese history.
6. Her speech __________ (使确信) us that hard work matters.
7. Confucius was a great Chinese __________ (哲学家).
8. The discovery was of great __________ (重要性).
9. Winning the prize was a __________ (里程碑) in her life.
10. The manager knows how to __________ (管理) a large team.
11. The new rule had a strong __________ (影响) on students' lives.
12. We should always tell the __________ (真相).

## 二、用括号内词的适当形式填空（每题 1 分）

13. The team performed __________ in the final. (outstanding)
14. This is a __________ novel in English literature. (classical)
15. He smiled __________ and continued his work. (ordinary)
16. I am __________ that he will come on time. (convince)
17. Her explanation was very __________ and easy to understand. (convince)
18. The book introduces the writer's __________ in a simple way. (philosopher)
19. The result has great __________ for the whole project. (significant)
20. Good time __________ is important for every student. (manage)
21. The story is __________ true. (true)
22. Please __________ on your pronunciation when reading aloud. (concentrated)

## 三、根据中文提示完成短语（每题 1 分）

23. __________ __________ to English, he can speak French.（除……之外）
24. My grandfather is __________ __________ __________ traditional opera.（喜欢）
25. The little girl __________ __________ the book on the top shelf.（伸手去拿）
26. They are __________ __________ equal rights.（为……而奋斗）
27. She chose the book with young readers __________ __________.（心中想着……）
28. Please __________ __________ your homework.（专注于）
29. She has a talent __________ painting.（有……的天赋）
30. Everyone can make a contribution __________ protecting the environment.（为……作贡献）
31. The scientist changed people's ideas __________ space.（改变人们对……的看法）
32. The school was named __________ a famous writer.（以……命名）
33. He is remembered __________ his kindness.（因……而被记住）
34. Marie Curie was born __________ a poor family.（出生于）

## 四、语境与句型填空（每题 1 分）

35. She __________ __________ in a small village and later became a doctor.（长大）
36. This machine is __________ __________ __________ many small parts.（由……组成）
37. I am afraid __________ I cannot agree with you.
38. He was unsure __________ he should do next.
39. It is kind __________ you to help the old man.
40. It is important __________ us to read every day.
41. I am not sure __________ the answer is correct.
42. We can learn a lot __________ great people.

## 五、补充短语默写（每题 1 分）

43. She spends two hours __________ English every day.（花时间做某事）
44. The scientist __________ __________ a new laboratory in 2020.（建立）
45. My sister is __________ __________ __________ science.（对……感兴趣）
