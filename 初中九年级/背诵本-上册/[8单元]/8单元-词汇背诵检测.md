# Unit 8 The power of love - 词汇背诵检测

建议用时：40 分钟。请先独立完成，再查看文末答案。

## 一、根据中文或首字母提示，写出恰当的单词（每题 1 分）

1. I cannot find my __________ (钱包).
2. The house was his most valuable __________ (财物).
3. She wore a red __________ (围巾) around her neck.
4. These __________ (剪刀) are too sharp for children.
5. He __________ (躺) on the bed and read a book yesterday.
6. Parents often make great __________ (牺牲) for their children.
7. It was a __________ (无私的) act of love.
8. Many children in __________ (贫困) need our help.
9. He works hard to __________ (赚得) enough money.
10. The lake __________ (结冰) in winter.
11. Your advice is very __________ (有价值的) to me.
12. She is __________ (慷慨的) with her time and money.

## 二、用括号内词的适当形式填空（每题 1 分）

13. The old watch is one of my favourite __________. (possess)
14. The shop sells woollen __________ in winter. (scarf)
15. Please give me the __________ on the table. (scissors)
16. The soldier made a great __________ for his country. (sacrifice)
17. The volunteers are kind and __________. (self)
18. The family lived in __________ for many years. (poor)
19. His monthly __________ are not high. (earn)
20. The road was covered with __________ snow. (freeze)
21. Time is more __________ than money. (value)
22. We thanked him for his __________. (generous)
23. I am __________ to you for your help. (gratitude)
24. It was a __________ act of love. (sacrifice)

## 三、根据中文提示完成短语（每题 1 分）

25. These stories are __________ __________ from parents to children.（传给后代）
26. I had no choice __________ __________ wait.（别无选择只能）
27. She __________ her eyes __________ the letter.（凝视）
28. He bought __________ __________ __________ books for his sister.（一套）
29. Please __________ the dirty dishes __________.（拿走）
30. The stranger __________ __________ __________ be our new teacher.（结果是）
31. We are __________ __________ our school.（为……感到骄傲）
32. The police __________ __________ the room carefully.（仔细搜寻）
33. He could not __________ a new coat.（买得起）
34. She __________ a letter __________ her bag.（从……取出）
35. The child __________ __________ his hand for help.（伸出）
36. I drank tea __________ __________ coffee.（而不是）

## 四、语境与句型填空（每题 1 分）

37. The new bike __________ me 800 yuan.（花费某人……）
38. Flowers can __________ your love __________ your mother.（表达对……的爱）
39. The nurse takes good care __________ the old man.
40. She is willing __________ __________ others.（帮助）
41. Never __________ __________ when you meet difficulties.（放弃）
42. I am grateful __________ my teacher __________ her advice.（因某事感谢某人）
43. He is good __________ playing basketball.
44. What is the reason __________ your absence?
45. Please wait __________ me at the school gate.
46. The box was __________ heavy __________ I could not lift it.（如此……以至于）
47. It was __________ a heavy box __________ I could not lift it.（如此……以至于）
48. He had no choice but __________ __________ the truth.（说出）
