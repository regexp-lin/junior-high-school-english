# Unit 6 Food and health - 词汇背诵检测

建议用时：35 分钟。请先独立完成，再查看文末答案。

## 一、根据中文或首字母提示，写出恰当的单词（每题 1 分）

1. __________ (煮沸) the water before you drink it.
2. Eating too much may make you __________ (超重的).
3. Candy and cola are usually __________ (含糖的).
4. Everyone has a food __________ (偏爱).
5. Good __________ (营养) is important for children.
6. Oranges are rich in __________ (维生素) C.
7. Eggs and fish contain a lot of __________ (蛋白质).
8. Rice and bread are kinds of __________ (碳水化合物).
9. A __________ (黄瓜) is often used in a salad.
10. A __________ (胡萝卜) is good for your eyes.
11. __________ (卷心菜) is a healthy vegetable.
12. Milk is an important __________ (乳制品).

## 二、用括号内词的适当形式填空（每题 1 分）

13. The potatoes are __________, so you can eat them now. (boil)
14. He is __________ because he eats too much fast food. (weight)
15. We should eat less __________ food. (sugar)
16. My __________ is tea rather than coffee. (prefer)
17. Fruit provides us with important __________. (nutritious)
18. Doctors say that __________ are necessary for our bodies. (vitamin)
19. Beans are a good source of __________. (protein)
20. Bread has a lot of __________. (carbohydrate)
21. This drink is too __________ for young children. (fizz)
22. Fried chicken is not as __________ as fresh vegetables. (health)

## 三、根据中文提示完成短语（每题 1 分）

23. We should have __________ __________ __________.（均衡饮食）
24. __________ __________, children need more sleep than adults.（总体上）
25. Try to eat less __________ __________.（油炸食品）
26. I __________ __________ stay at home than go out in the rain.（宁愿）
27. This song reminds me __________ my childhood.（使某人想起）
28. You should __________ __________ __________ junk food.（远离）
29. He exercises every day to __________ __________.（减肥）
30. Broccoli is __________ __________ vitamins.（富含）
31. Fish is a good __________ __________ protein.（……的来源）
32. We should __________ __________ before going shopping.（计划饮食）
33. Too much sugar is bad __________ your teeth.
34. You need to __________ __________ __________ soft drinks.（减少）

## 四、语境与句型填空（每题 1 分）

35. Eating vegetables is good __________ your health.
36. I don't know what I __________ eat for breakfast.（应该）
37. I would rather __________ water than __________ cola.（喝）
38. My sister prefers milk __________ juice.
39. We prefer __________ at home to __________ out.（吃；外出就餐）
40. He __________ __________ weight after the holiday.（增加体重）
41. It took her a long time to __________ __________ the illness.（度过）
42. I think __________ a balanced diet is necessary.
