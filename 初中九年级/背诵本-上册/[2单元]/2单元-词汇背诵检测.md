# Unit 2 Great ideas - 词汇背诵检测

建议用时：35 分钟。请先独立完成，再查看文末答案。

## 一、根据中文或首字母提示，写出恰当的单词（每题 1 分）

1. He held a __________ (坚定的) belief that everyone could learn.
2. __________ (无知) can cause many misunderstandings.
3. Please fill in the __________ (申请表) carefully.
4. The children were __________ (满意的) with the trip.
5. The new road will __________ (取代) the old one.
6. Don't be a __________ (傻瓜) and give up so easily.
7. A healthy diet should be __________ (均衡的).
8. Can you __________ (证明) that the story is true?
9. Learning a language is a __________ (挑战).
10. The shop sells __________ (各种各样的) books.
11. Please give me a __________ (明确的) answer.
12. I could __________ (几乎不) believe my eyes.

## 二、用括号内词的适当形式填空（每题 1 分）

13. She spoke with great __________. (firm)
14. His __________ of local customs made others unhappy. (ignorant)
15. We need to __________ for a new passport. (application)
16. The teacher was pleased with the students' __________. (satisfy)
17. The new rule led to the __________ of many workers. (displace)
18. It was __________ of me to trust a stranger. (fool)
19. Keeping a __________ between work and rest is important. (balanced)
20. There is no __________ that he was at home then. (prove)
21. This task is __________, but we can finish it. (challenge)
22. The answer is __________ correct. (definite)

## 三、根据中文提示完成短语（每题 1 分）

23. We are __________ __________ the result.（对……满意）
24. Please __________ the bottle __________ water.（用……装满）
25. The water is __________ __________ the edge of the cup.（溢出）
26. Go __________ __________ the end of the road.（径直走向）
27. We should consider the problem __________ __________ __________.（作为整体）
28. The teacher tried to __________ the idea __________ to us.（把……讲清楚）
29. The desk is __________ __________ wood.（由……制成）
30. Your bag is the same colour __________ mine.（与……相同）
31. The judge sent the thief __________ __________.（送进监狱）
32. The coach praised her __________ her hard work.（因……表扬）
33. Can you __________ __________ __________ a better plan?（想出）
34. The accident __________ __________ because of careless driving.（发生）

## 四、语境与句型填空（每题 1 分）

35. They are trying to __________ a difficult problem.
36. We found __________ that the museum was closed on Monday.
37. He discovered the answer __________ __________.（偶然）
38. Reading can __________ __________ new ideas.（导致）
39. She is an expert in the field __________ medicine.
40. __________ you ready for the test?（用 be 动词构成一般疑问句）
41. __________ make so much noise in the library.（祈使句否定）
42. __________ a beautiful day it is!（用 What 构成感叹句）
