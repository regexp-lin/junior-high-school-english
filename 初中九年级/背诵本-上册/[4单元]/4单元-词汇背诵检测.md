# Unit 4 A better me - 词汇背诵检测

建议用时：35 分钟。请先独立完成，再查看文末答案。

## 一、根据中文或首字母提示，写出恰当的单词（每题 1 分）

1. Students should __________ (合理使用) their time wisely.
2. Too much __________ (压力) is bad for our health.
3. Some teenagers are __________ (上瘾的) to online games.
4. We should stop __________ (欺凌) at school.
5. The monitor is in __________ (负责) of the class meeting.
6. The weather was __________ (糟糕的) yesterday.
7. Please __________ (集中注意力) on the speaker.
8. Loud noise can __________ (影响) your sleep.
9. He __________ (讨厌) being late.
10. I felt __________ (尴尬的) when I gave the wrong answer.
11. Your room is in a __________ (杂乱) again.
12. She didn't __________ (回应) to my message.

## 二、用括号内词的适当形式填空（每题 1 分）

13. The workers were under great __________. (press)
14. His computer game __________ worried his parents. (addict)
15. The teacher asked him to stay __________. (focus)
16. The speech had a great __________ on us. (affect)
17. Her face showed __________ after the mistake. (embarrass)
18. The room looks __________ after the party. (mess)
19. We are waiting for the doctor's __________. (respond)
20. She felt __________ of her rude words. (shame)
21. He felt __________ before the interview. (anxiety)
22. Practice can make you more __________. (confidence)
23. Every student should be __________ for their own work. (responsibility)
24. Living alone helps young people become __________. (independence)

## 三、根据中文提示完成短语（每题 1 分）

25. Don't __________ __________ __________ mobile phones.（沉迷于）
26. Who is __________ __________ __________ the project?（负责）
27. You should __________ __________ your study.（专注于）
28. The noise is __________ me __________.（使某人发疯）
29. Don't __________ the experiment __________.（把……弄糟）
30. I __________ __________ __________ with my new classmates.（与……相处融洽）
31. He felt __________ __________ __________ his mistake.（为……感到羞愧）
32. You can __________ your teacher __________ advice.（向……寻求）
33. My parents often __________ me useful advice.（给某人建议）
34. We must learn to __________ __________ stress.（处理）

## 四、语境与句型填空（每题 1 分）

35. She has difficulty __________ English articles.（阅读）
36. When you are upset, you can __________ __________ your parents for help.（向……求助）
37. He is worried __________ his final exam.
38. It takes time to __________ friends __________ new people.（与……交朋友）
39. We should __________ __________ __________ ourselves.（严格要求）
40. The news made everyone __________.（开心）
41. Please give __________ the book, or give the book __________ me.（双宾语）
42. There __________ two books and a pen on the desk.（there be 句型）

## 五、补充短语默写（每题 1 分）

43. I __________ __________ __________ my new neighbour.（与……相处融洽）
44. You should __________ __________ study independently.（学会做某事）
45. Let's __________ __________ __________ after class.（休息一下）
