# Unit 5 My time, my joy - 词汇背诵检测

建议用时：35 分钟。请先独立完成，再查看文末答案。

## 一、根据中文或首字母提示，写出恰当的单词（每题 1 分）

1. Steven Spielberg is a famous film __________ (导演).
2. The programme was recorded in a television __________ (演播室).
3. Each __________ (参赛者) answered three questions.
4. Our school will __________ (举办) a singing competition.
5. Thank you for your useful __________ (推荐).
6. The film has an exciting __________ (情节).
7. Climbing the mountain is __________ (有挑战性的).
8. Our __________ (目标) is to win the match.
9. The __________ (摄影师) is taking photos of the stars.
10. People have __________ (各种各样的) hobbies.
11. The TV __________ (主持人) welcomed the guests.
12. The team celebrated its __________ (胜利).

## 二、用括号内词的适当形式填空（每题 1 分）

13. There were three __________ working on the film. (cameraman)
14. The teacher gave us several __________ about good books. (recommendation)
15. This game is more __________ than the last one. (challenge)
16. The club __________ to help students enjoy reading. (aim)
17. The programme is __________ by a local company. (conduct)
18. The famous __________ interviewed the winner after the show. (host)
19. The team's __________ made everyone excited. (victory)
20. There are many different film __________, such as comedy and drama. (genre)
21. She is __________ in music and art. (interest)
22. My brother enjoys __________ TV programmes. (watch)

## 三、根据中文提示完成短语（每题 1 分）

23. The sun was shining __________ __________ us.（照在……身上）
24. The patient __________ __________ because of the heat.（昏迷）
25. China has several __________ __________.（时区）
26. We often __________ __________ at the park after school.（闲逛）
27. I read a __________ __________ before watching the film.（影评）
28. More than one hundred students __________ __________ __________ the race.（参加）
29. Our runner was far __________ __________ the others.（领先于）
30. Please __________ __________ while I take the photo.（保持不动）
31. She worked hard and __________ __________ __________.（获奖）
32. I __________ __________ __________ reading stories.（对……感兴趣）
33. I __________ tea __________ coffee.（比起……更喜欢……）
34. We spent two hours __________ the classroom.（打扫）

## 四、语境与句型填空（每题 1 分）

35. __________ he was tired, he continued working.（虽然）
36. Although he was tired, he continued working. 此句中不可以再与 __________ 连用。
37. The film was __________ interesting __________ we watched it twice.（如此……以至于）
38. It was __________ an interesting film __________ we watched it twice.（如此……以至于）
39. She enjoys __________ in the school band.（演奏）
40. He is interested in __________ model planes.（制作）
41. I prefer __________ to __________.（游泳；跑步）
42. They spent the afternoon __________ for the show.（练习）

## 五、补充节目词汇（每题 1 分）

43. The __________ __________ adjusts the lights during a programme.（灯光师）
44. The __________-__________ __________ makes actors look ready for the camera.（化妆师）
45. The students watched a __________ __________ on TV.（智力竞赛节目）
