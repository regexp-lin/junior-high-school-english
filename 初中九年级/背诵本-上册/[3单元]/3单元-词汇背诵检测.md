# Unit 3 Family traditions - 词汇背诵检测

建议用时：35 分钟。请先独立完成，再查看文末答案。

## 一、根据中文或首字母提示，写出恰当的单词（每题 1 分）

1. It is a __________ (可耻的) thing to cheat in an exam.
2. Please __________ (移开) your bag from the chair.
3. Nobody likes a __________ (骗子).
4. It was a difficult __________ (决定) to leave home.
5. Exercise can __________ (增强) your body.
6. There was an __________ (尴尬的) silence after his joke.
7. Her __________ (笑声) filled the room.
8. A __________ (眼泪) rolled down her face.
9. Honesty is an important part of __________ (道德准则).
10. We watched the dancer with __________ (钦佩).
11. The boy repaired the bike __________ (熟练地).
12. The Spring Festival is a Chinese __________ (传统).

## 二、用括号内词的适当形式填空（每题 1 分）

13. He felt great __________ after telling a lie. (ashamed)
14. The __________ of the old bridge will start tomorrow. (remove)
15. The __________ finally admitted that he had copied the answers. (cheat)
16. We need to __________ whether to go now. (decision)
17. Regular practice will build your __________. (strong)
18. She answered the question __________. (awkward)
19. The children __________ happily in the garden. (laughter)
20. The teacher's words were greatly __________ by the students. (admiration)
21. The artist painted the picture very __________. (skillful)
22. We celebrate many __________ festivals every year. (tradition)

## 三、根据中文提示完成短语（每题 1 分）

23. The children looked at the hero __________ __________.（怀着钦佩）
24. Parents should teach children about __________ __________ __________.（是非）
25. That exciting trip is still __________ in my memory.（记忆犹新）
26. Tom __________ __________ __________ his best friend yesterday.（与……闹翻）
27. We went __________ on the island last summer.（钓鱼）
28. There is a small house __________ the middle of the forest.（在……中间）
29. Please __________ a match; it is too dark here.（点燃）
30. Put the book __________ on the shelf after reading it.（放回）
31. Don't __________ __________ __________ the rope.（放开）
32. The team came __________ __________ many difficulties.（遭遇）
33. She __________ the bed sheets every Friday.（换床单）
34. You should __________ your ideas __________ your parents.（和……分享）

## 四、语境与句型填空（每题 1 分）

35. Good friends should __________ __________ each other.（沟通）
36. Can you __________ __________ __________?（保守秘密）
37. It is time to __________ __________ __________.（作决定）
38. We can __________ __________ our mistakes.（从……中学习）
39. The soup __________ delicious.（尝起来）
40. Her face __________ red when she heard the news.（变得）
41. Please __________ quiet while the baby is sleeping.（保持）
42. Every time I see the old photo, I __________ of my grandmother.

## 五、补充短语默写（每题 1 分）

43. Her parents are __________ __________ her excellent work.（为……感到骄傲）
