# Unit 7 Reading Mark Twain - 词汇背诵检测

建议用时：40 分钟。请先独立完成，再查看文末答案。

## 一、根据中文或首字母提示，写出恰当的单词（每题 1 分）

1. We __________ (把……联系起来) Mark Twain with his famous novels.
2. His books are full of __________ (幽默).
3. The boat __________ (下沉) slowly into the river.
4. The company carried out a __________ (调查) last month.
5. Please read the poem __________ (大声地).
6. The old __________ (绅士) opened the door for the lady.
7. Her parents are __________ (担心的) about her health.
8. Failure is not the end; it can lead to __________ (成功).
9. The children went on an __________ (冒险).
10. Huck is an important __________ (人物) in the novel.
11. The story has an exciting __________ (情节).
12. The novel's __________ (背景) is a small town by a river.

## 二、用括号内词的适当形式填空（每题 1 分）

13. There is a strong __________ between the two events. (associate)
14. He is a very __________ writer. (humour)
15. The ship has __________ to the bottom of the sea. (sink)
16. The two __________ were polite and helpful. (gentleman)
17. This problem is of great __________ to us. (concern)
18. His first book was a __________, but he did not give up. (fail)
19. She loves stories with __________ heroes. (adventure)
20. The author created many unforgettable __________. (character)
21. The film is __________ on a true story. (base)
22. The meeting will __________ at 9 a.m. tomorrow. (place)

## 三、根据中文提示完成短语（每题 1 分）

23. The novel is written from the __________-__________ point of view.（第一人称）
24. Please __________ __________ the book from the floor.（捡起）
25. We should __________ __________ __________ our work.（继续）
26. The rain __________ __________ all night.（持续）
27. My sister is __________ __________ __________ her clothes.（对……挑剔）
28. The boy walked __________ __________ __________ in his step.（迈着轻快的步伐）
29. He hopes to __________ __________ __________ by writing.（发财）
30. I will __________ you __________ next week.（偿还）
31. Mark Twain is __________ __________ one of the greatest American writers.（被认为是）
32. He __________ __________ in a poor family.（长大）
33. She decided to __________ __________ __________ as a teacher.（找工作）
34. Many people went west to __________ __________ gold.（淘金）

## 四、语境与句型填空（每题 1 分）

35. The writer __________ a book when he was thirty.（出版）
36. He often gives __________ about his travels.（演讲）
37. The author put his experiences __________ __________.（写进作品）
38. Mark Twain is famous __________ his humour.
39. The film is based __________ a famous novel.
40. The story __________ __________ in a small town.（发生）
41. The boy __________ __________ a trick on Tom.（捉弄）
42. He pretended __________ __________ asleep.（假装）
43. This is the book __________ I bought yesterday.（关系代词）
44. It __________ me two hours to finish the book.（花费）

## 五、定语从句补充（每题 1 分）

45. The writer __________ lives in that house is very humorous.（关系代词）
