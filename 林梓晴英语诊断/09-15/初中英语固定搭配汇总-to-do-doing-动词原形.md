# 初中英语固定搭配汇总：to do、doing 与动词原形

**整理日期：2026 年 9 月 15 日**  
**适用对象：初中英语学习与九年级复习，重点针对 ask sb. do、without do 这类错误。**

## 一、这类题究竟考什么

核心是：**根据前面的词和完整句型，判断后面的动词用 to do、doing 还是原形。**

| 形式 | 初中常用知识点名称 | 示例 | 怎样理解 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| to do | 动词不定式 | ask me **to read** | 要求我读；to read 补充说明要求我做什么 |
| doing | 动名词 | without **reading** | 没有读；reading 作介词 without 的宾语 |
| do | 不带 to 的动词不定式 | let me **read** | 允许我读；let 后的这个结构省去 to |
| doing | 现在分词 | see him **reading** | 看见他正在读；reading 说明当时正在进行的动作 |

**注意：doing 是形式名称，不等于每个 doing 都是动名词，也不等于现在进行时。** 本文按做题时需要填写的形式分类。情态动词、助动词后的原形也列入对照，但不把所有原形用法都统称为同一种非谓语结构。

### 范围与符号

- 本文尽量覆盖初中常见的相关搭配和句型；不同教材的收录范围不同，它不是某一地区考试的官方穷尽清单。
- **核心**部分优先掌握；标为**补充**的内容用于遇题查阅、拓展辨析。
- `sb.` = somebody，某人；`sth.` = something，某事／某物；`do` 代表动词原形，可以替换为 read、go、help 等。
- `be` 要随主语和时态变为 am/is/are/was/were 等；括号中的成分通常表示可以省略，但仍须看具体句型。
- 表中列出的是**所示意义下的常见结构**，不表示这个词在所有句子里只能这样使用。
- 例句与练习均为自编。语法依据链接放在相关说明旁，便于进一步查阅。

## 二、先掌握一张判断表

| 看到的线索 | 优先想到的结构 | 示例 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ask/tell/want + 某人 | sb. to do | asked me **to wait** |
| want/hope/decide 等后直接接动作 | to do | decided **to leave** |
| enjoy/finish/practise 等后直接接动作 | doing | finished **reading** |
| without/by/for 等介词后直接接动作作宾语 | doing | without **speaking** |
| look forward to/be used to 中的介词 to | doing | look forward to **seeing** you |
| 主动句中的 let/make + 某人 | do | let me **go** |
| help + 某人 | do 或 to do | help me **carry/to carry** it |
| see/hear/watch + 某人 | do 或 doing，结合意思选 | saw him **cross/crossing** the road |
| stop/remember/forget/try 等 | to do 和 doing 意思不同 | stop **talking** / stop **to talk** |

**记住：不能单靠中文“让”“做”“去”判断英语结构。ask me to go 与 let me go 都可能译为“让我去”，但形式不同。**

## 三、第一类：动词 + sb. + to do sth.

这类结构中，to do 常作**宾语补足语**：补充说明“某人要做什么”。ask、tell 等接“某人 + to do”的用法可参见 [British Council：Verbs followed by the infinitive](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/english-grammar-reference/verbs-followed-infinitive)。

### 3.1 核心搭配

| 固定搭配 | 中文意思 | 自编例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ask sb. to do sth. | 请求／要求某人做某事 | Dad asked me **to close** the window. |
| tell sb. to do sth. | 告诉／嘱咐某人做某事 | Our teacher told us **to wait** outside. |
| want sb. to do sth. | 想让某人做某事 | I want you **to join** our team. |
| would like sb. to do sth. | 想请某人做某事 | I'd like you **to read** this story. |
| invite sb. to do sth. | 邀请某人做某事 | Amy invited me **to visit** her school. |
| teach sb. to do sth. | 教某人做某事 | My uncle taught me **to swim**. |
| advise sb. to do sth. | 建议某人做某事 | The doctor advised him **to rest**. |
| allow sb. to do sth. | 允许某人做某事 | Mum allows me **to use** her computer. |
| encourage sb. to do sth. | 鼓励某人做某事 | They encouraged me **to try** again. |
| expect sb. to do sth. | 期待／预计某人做某事 | We expect him **to arrive** soon. |
| remind sb. to do sth. | 提醒某人做某事 | Please remind me **to bring** my notebook. |
| warn sb. not to do sth. | 警告某人不要做某事 | Dad warned us **not to touch** the wire. |

### 3.2 补充搭配

| 固定搭配 | 中文意思 | 自编例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| get sb. to do sth. | 设法让某人做某事 | I got my brother **to help** me. |
| order sb. to do sth. | 命令某人做某事 | The officer ordered them **to leave**. |
| force sb. to do sth. | 强迫某人做某事 | They forced him **to give up** the game. |
| persuade sb. to do sth. | 说服某人做某事 | We persuaded her **to join** the club. |
| wish sb. to do sth. | 希望某人做某事，较正式 | I wish you **to stay** a little longer. |

### 3.3 三个必须分清的地方

**① 否定后面的动作：sb. + not to do。**

- The teacher asked us **not to run**. 老师要求我们不要跑。
- The teacher **didn't ask** us to run. 老师没有要求我们跑。

两句的否定对象不同：前一句要求“不跑”；后一句没有提出“跑”的要求。

**② 时态变在前面的谓语上，不把 to 后的动词改成过去式。**

- 正确：Yesterday, Dad **asked** me **to clean** my room.
- 错误：Yesterday, Dad asked me **to cleaned** my room.

**③ 不要把相似词强行套成同一结构。**

| 想表达的意思 | 正确结构 | 不采用的结构 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 希望他获胜 | I hope **he wins**. | I hope him to win. |
| 建议他休息 | I advise **him to rest**. | I suggest him to rest. |
| 建议休息一下 | I suggest **taking a break**. | I suggest to take a break. |

## 四、第二类：动词 + to do sth.

这里通常是句子主语自己要做后面的动作。例如 I want to read. 中，想读书的是 I。

### 4.1 核心搭配

| 固定搭配 | 中文意思 | 自编例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| want to do | 想做 | I want **to learn** French. |
| would like to do | 想做，语气较委婉 | I'd like **to have** some water. |
| hope to do | 希望做 | We hope **to win** the match. |
| wish to do | 希望做，较正式 | I wish **to speak** to the manager. |
| decide to do | 决定做 | She decided **to walk** home. |
| plan to do | 计划做 | We plan **to visit** the museum. |
| learn to do | 学习做 | He is learning **to cook**. |
| agree to do | 同意做 | They agreed **to help** us. |
| promise to do | 答应做 | I promised **to return** the book. |
| refuse to do | 拒绝做 | She refused **to give up**. |
| choose to do | 选择做 | He chose **to stay** at home. |
| offer to do | 主动提出做 | Amy offered **to carry** the box. |
| expect to do | 期待／预计做 | I expect **to finish** before six. |
| need to do | 需要做 | You need **to check** your answer. |
| afford to do | 有能力／有钱做，常与 can/could 连用 | We can't afford **to buy** a new car. |
| manage to do | 设法成功做到 | We managed **to catch** the last bus. |
| fail to do | 未能做到 | He failed **to catch** the train. |
| prepare to do | 准备做 | The students are preparing **to leave**. |

### 4.2 补充搭配

| 固定搭配 | 中文意思 | 自编例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| seem to do | 似乎做／是 | She seems **to know** the answer. |
| happen to do | 碰巧做／是 | I happened **to meet** him at the station. |
| volunteer to do | 自愿做 | We volunteered **to clean** the park. |
| can't wait to do | 迫不及待做 | I can't wait **to open** the gift. |
| do/try one's best to do | 尽某人最大努力做 | I'll do my best **to help** you. |
| make up one's mind to do | 下定决心做 | She made up her mind **to study** harder. |
| make an effort to do | 努力做 | He made an effort **to speak** clearly. |

**对照：** I want **to go**.（我想去。）／I want **you to go**.（我想让你去。）注意动作执行者是否变化。

## 五、第三类：含 to do 的常见句型

这些句型不一定都是“动词后固定接不定式”，还包括不定式作主语、定语、目的状语等用法。先掌握整句意思和形式。

| 句型 | 中文意思／用法 | 自编例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| be happy/glad/pleased to do | 很高兴做 | I'm glad **to meet** you. |
| be sorry to do | 对要说／得知的事情感到抱歉或难过等 | I'm sorry **to hear** that. |
| be ready to do | 准备好做／愿意做 | We are ready **to start**. |
| be willing to do | 愿意做 | She is willing **to help**. |
| be able to do | 能够做 | He is able **to swim** now. |
| be supposed to do | 按规定／约定应该做 | We are supposed **to arrive** on time. |
| be expected to do | 被期望／被要求做 | Students are expected **to follow** the rules. |
| be about to do | 正要／即将做 | We were about **to leave** when the phone rang. |
| be sure to do | 一定会做；祈使表达中可指务必做 | Be sure **to close** the door. |
| be likely to do（补充） | 很可能做 | It is likely **to rain** tonight. |
| It's time to do | 到了做某事的时间 | It's time **to go** home. |
| It's time for sb. to do | 到了某人做某事的时间 | It's time for us **to leave**. |
| It takes sb. some time to do | 做某事花费某人一段时间 | It takes me ten minutes **to walk** to school. |
| It is + adj. + for sb. to do | 对某人来说，做某事是……的 | It is important for us **to exercise**. |
| It is + adj. + of sb. to do | 某人这样做，体现其品格／行为特点 | It is kind of you **to help** me. |
| find/think it + adj. + to do | 发现／认为做某事是……的 | I find it useful **to take** notes. |
| too + adj./adv. + to do | 太……而不能做 | The boy is too young **to drive**. |
| adj./adv. + enough + to do | 足够……可以做 | She is old enough **to travel** alone. |
| enough + n. + to do | 有足够的……来做 | We have enough time **to finish**. |
| 疑问词 + to do | 如何做／做什么／去哪里等 | I don't know how **to solve** it. |
| something/anything/nothing + to do | 要做的事／可用来做的东西等 | I have something **to tell** you. |
| 名词 + to do | 用不定式修饰名词 | She has a chance **to win**. |
| to do / in order to do | 为了做，表示目的 | I got up early **to catch** the bus. |
| so as to do（补充） | 为了做，表示目的 | He spoke slowly so as **to make** himself clear. |

### 5.1 for 与 of 怎样区分

- **for sb.**：形容词主要评价“做这件事”的难易、重要性等，如 easy、difficult、important、necessary。
- **of sb.**：形容词主要评价“某人这样做”的品格或行为，如 kind、nice、polite、careless。

对照：It is difficult **for me** to finish it.／It is kind **of you** to help me.

### 5.2 两个小细节

- **what to do**：做什么；**how to do it**：怎样做这件事。do 在后者中需要有明确的宾语 it。
- **a room to live in / a pen to write with**：末尾介词有作用，不能随意删掉。I need a pen **to write with**. 表示“我需要一支用来写字的笔”。

## 六、第四类：动词或固定表达 + doing sth.

enjoy、finish、practise、mind 等后接动作时常用 -ing。相关动词与“动词 + 某人 + -ing”的分类可参见 [British Council：Verbs followed by the -ing form](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/english-grammar-reference/verbs-followed-ing-form)。

### 6.1 核心搭配

| 固定搭配 | 中文意思 | 自编例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| enjoy doing | 喜欢／享受做 | I enjoy **reading** stories. |
| finish doing | 完成做 | She finished **writing** the report. |
| practise/practice doing | 练习做；前者是英式动词拼写 | We practise **speaking** English daily. |
| mind doing | 介意做 | Do you mind **opening** the window? |
| avoid doing | 避免做 | Try to avoid **making** the same mistake. |
| consider doing | 考虑做 | We are considering **moving** house. |
| suggest doing | 建议做 | He suggested **taking** a bus. |
| keep doing | 持续做／反复做 | Keep **trying**! |
| keep on doing | 继续／反复做 | She kept on **asking** questions. |
| give up doing | 放弃做 | Don't give up **learning** English. |
| feel like doing | 想要做 | I feel like **taking** a walk. |
| can't help doing | 忍不住做 | We couldn't help **laughing**. |
| put off doing | 推迟做 | Don't put off **doing** your homework. |
| be busy doing | 忙于做 | Mum is busy **cooking** dinner. |
| be worth doing | 值得做 | This book is worth **reading**. |
| spend time/money (in) doing | 花时间／金钱做 | She spends an hour **reading** every day. |
| have fun doing | 做某事很开心 | We had fun **playing** outside. |
| have a good/great time doing | 做某事很愉快 | They had a great time **visiting** the farm. |
| have trouble/difficulty (in) doing | 做某事有困难 | I have difficulty **understanding** him. |

### 6.2 补充搭配与活动表达

| 固定搭配 | 中文意思 | 自编例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| imagine doing | 想象做 | Can you imagine **living** on the moon? |
| miss doing | 错过做／怀念做，视语境而定 | I miss **playing** with my old friends. |
| dislike doing | 不喜欢做 | He dislikes **waiting** in long lines. |
| risk doing | 冒险做 | Don't risk **losing** your ticket. |
| end up doing | 最后做了 | We ended up **staying** at home. |
| have problems (in) doing | 做某事有困难 | He has problems **remembering** names. |
| have a hard time doing | 做某事很费劲 | She had a hard time **finding** the station. |
| go shopping/swimming/fishing/skating/hiking | 去购物／游泳／钓鱼／滑冰／远足 | We went **hiking** on Sunday. |

**go doing 主要用于约定俗成的休闲、运动等活动表达，不能把所有“去做某事”都写成 go doing。** “去买牛奶”可以写 go to buy some milk，不能机械写 go buying some milk。

### 6.3 两个特别提醒

- **Would you mind not doing…?** 表示“请你不要……好吗？”例如 Would you mind **not talking** so loudly?
- **be worth doing** 中，doing 常表达被动含义：The book is worth **reading**. = 这本书值得被阅读。学习这一常见句型时用 worth reading。

## 七、第五类：介词 + doing sth.

**在下列结构中，动作直接作介词的宾语时，用 doing。** 介词还可以接名词、代词，不能把规则背成“介词后只能接 doing”。

### 7.1 从 without 迁移到其他介词

| 结构 | 中文意思 | 自编例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| without doing | 没有做／在不做的情况下 | She left without **saying** goodbye. |
| by doing | 通过做，表示方法 | He learns English by **watching** videos. |
| before doing | 做……之前 | Wash your hands before **eating**. |
| after doing | 做……之后 | We went home after **finishing** the work. |
| for doing | 因为做／用于做，结合语境 | Thank you for **helping** me. |
| about doing | 关于做 | We talked about **joining** the club. |
| instead of doing | 代替做／而不是做 | He walked instead of **taking** a bus. |

without 后接 -ing 的用法和意义参见 [Cambridge：Without](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/without)。

**before/after 也能引导完整从句：** after **finishing** the work 与 after **we finished** the work 都可以。后一种有主语 we，要使用适合语境的谓语形式，不能机械地见 after 就填 doing。

### 7.2 形容词／动词短语中的介词

| 固定搭配 | 中文意思 | 自编例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| be good at doing | 擅长做 | She is good at **drawing**. |
| do well in doing | 在做某事方面表现好 | He does well in **solving** maths problems. |
| be interested in doing | 对做某事感兴趣 | I'm interested in **learning** Chinese. |
| be afraid of doing | 害怕做／担心发生某事 | She is afraid of **making** mistakes. |
| be proud of doing | 为做某事感到自豪 | He is proud of **helping** others. |
| be tired of doing | 厌烦做 | I'm tired of **waiting**. |
| be worried about doing | 担心做 | She is worried about **missing** the bus. |
| be excited about doing | 对做某事感到兴奋 | We're excited about **visiting** the zoo. |
| be sure of/about doing | 对做某事有把握 | He is sure of **winning** the game. |
| be used for doing | 被用于做 | This knife is used for **cutting** bread. |
| thank sb. for doing | 因某人做了某事而感谢他／她 | I thanked her for **helping** me. |
| be sorry for doing | 因做了某事而抱歉 | I'm sorry for **breaking** your cup. |
| apologize to sb. for doing | 因某事向某人道歉 | He apologized to me for **being** late. |
| think about/of doing | 考虑做 | I'm thinking about **joining** the team. |
| dream of/about doing | 梦想做 | She dreams of **becoming** a pilot. |
| succeed in doing | 成功做成 | We succeeded in **finding** the key. |
| stop/prevent sb. from doing | 阻止某人做 | The rain stopped us from **going** out. |
| keep sb. from doing | 阻止某人做 | The noise kept me from **sleeping**. |
| spend time/money on doing | 花时间／金钱做 | He spends time on **repairing** old bikes. |
| How/What about doing…? | 做……怎么样？ | How about **going** for a walk? |

**补充可查：** insist on doing（坚持做）等也应按完整搭配判断。初次学习先掌握上表，不必同时扩展所有低频短语。

## 八、第六类：to 是介词，后面接 doing

不能把“看到 to 就用原形”当成万能规则。**to 有时是不定式标记，有时是介词。** look forward to 中的 to 是介词，后接动作时用 -ing，参见 [Cambridge：Look forward to](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/look-forward-to)。

| 固定搭配 | 中文意思 | 自编例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| look forward to doing | 期待做 | I look forward to **seeing** you. |
| be used to doing | 习惯于做，强调状态 | I'm used to **getting** up early. |
| get used to doing | 逐渐习惯做，强调变化 | He is getting used to **living** here. |
| pay attention to doing | 注意做 | Pay attention to **spelling** words correctly. |
| prefer doing A to doing B | 比起做 B，更喜欢做 A | I prefer **walking** to **taking** a bus. |
| stick to doing（补充） | 坚持做 | She sticks to **exercising** every morning. |
| devote time/oneself to doing（补充） | 把时间／精力投入做 | He devotes his free time to **helping** others. |
| make a contribution to doing（补充） | 为做某事作出贡献 | We can make a contribution to **protecting** the environment. |

### 必会辨析：四种 used 结构

| 结构 | 意思 | 自编例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| used to do | 过去常常做／过去处于某种状态，通常暗示现在不同 | I used to **walk** to school. 我过去常步行上学。 |
| be/get used to doing | 习惯于／逐渐习惯做 | I'm used to **walking** to school. 我习惯步行上学。 |
| be used to do | 被用来做 | This tool is used to **cut** glass. 这个工具被用来切割玻璃。 |
| be used for doing | 被用于做 | This tool is used for **cutting** glass. 这个工具被用于切割玻璃。 |

**区别在含义和整体结构。** be used to 既可能表示“习惯于”，也可能是 use 的被动结构加目的不定式，不能只看这三个词就决定填 doing。

## 九、第七类：后接动词原形

### 9.1 使役动词与 help

| 结构 | 中文意思 | 自编例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| let sb. do | 允许／让某人做 | Let me **try**. |
| make sb. do | 使／迫使某人做 | The joke made us **laugh**. |
| have sb. do（补充） | 安排／让某人做 | I'll have Tom **call** you. |
| help sb. do / help sb. to do | 帮助某人做；两种形式均可 | She helped me **carry/to carry** the box. |
| help do / help to do | 帮忙做 | This exercise helps **improve/to improve** your memory. |

**make 的主动与被动要对照记：**

- 主动：The teacher made him **rewrite** the sentence.
- 被动：He was made **to rewrite** the sentence.

表示“被允许做”，通常使用 **be allowed to do**：We were allowed **to leave** early. 不要机械造出 be let to do。make 的被动恢复 to、允许意义用 allow 的说明参见 [British Council：Verbs followed by the infinitive](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/english-grammar-reference/verbs-followed-infinitive)。

### 9.2 建议、选择与基础原形结构

| 结构 | 中文意思／说明 | 自编例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| had better do | 最好做 | You'd better **leave** now. |
| had better not do | 最好不要做 | You'd better not **be** late. |
| would rather do | 宁愿做 | I'd rather **stay** at home. |
| would rather not do | 宁愿不做 | I'd rather not **go** out. |
| would rather do A than do B | 宁愿做 A，也不愿做 B | I'd rather **walk** than **wait**. |
| Why not do…? | 为什么不做……呢？ | Why not **ask** your teacher? |
| Why don't you/we do…? | 为什么你／我们不做……呢？ | Why don't we **take** a break? |
| Let's (not) do | 我们一起做／不要做 | Let's **start**. / Let's not **argue**. |
| can/could/may/might/must/should/will/would + do | 情态动词后接原形 | You should **check** your answer. |
| do/does/did 作助动词 + 动词原形 | 疑问、否定等结构中还原动词 | Did he **go**? / She doesn't **know**. |
| 祈使句以动词原形开头 | 表示请求、命令等 | **Open** your book. / Don't **run**. |

**补充对照：** have to **go**、ought to **go** 含 to，应记完整结构。need 作实义动词用 **need to do**；作情态动词常见 **needn't do**：You needn't **worry**. = You don't need **to worry**.

## 十、第八类：感官动词后用 do 还是 doing

### 10.1 看到／听到的是什么

| 结构 | 常见侧重点 | 自编例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| see/hear/watch/notice sb. do | 把动作作为整体来观察，或表示反复发生的动作 | I saw him **cross** the road. 我看见他穿过马路。 |
| see/hear/watch/notice sb. doing | 强调看到／听到时动作正在进行 | I saw him **crossing** the road. 我看见他正在过马路。 |
| feel sb./sth. do/doing（补充） | 感觉到动作整体／正在发生 | I felt the house **shaking**. 我感觉房子在摇晃。 |

初中常用“全过程／正在进行”帮助理解，但不要把 **do** 绝对化为“必须从头到尾目睹每个细节”。没有足够语境时，两种形式可能都符合语法。

### 10.2 补充：被动句与相邻结构

- 主动：Someone saw him **enter** the room.
- 被动：He was seen **to enter** the room.
- 表示正在发生：He was seen **entering** the room.

另外两组常见搭配：

- **find/catch sb. doing**：发现／撞见某人正在做。I found him **sleeping** on the sofa.
- **keep/leave sb. doing**：让某人持续处于做某事的状态。Sorry to keep you **waiting**.

注意 **keep sb. doing**（让某人一直做）与 **keep sb. from doing**（阻止某人做）意思不同。

## 十一、第九类：to do 与 doing 都能用，但要看意思

remember、forget、try、stop 等后接两种形式时，意义会变化，参见 [British Council：Verbs followed by -ing or infinitive to change meaning](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/b1-b2/verbs-followed-ing-or-infinitive-change-meaning)。以下按初中做题需要整理。

### 11.1 必会的意思变化

| 结构对照 | 意思区别 | 自编例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| stop doing / stop to do | 停止正在做的事／停下原来的事去做另一件事 | Stop **talking**. 停止说话。／We stopped **to rest**. 我们停下来休息。 |
| remember to do / remember doing | 记得去做／记得以前做过 | Remember **to lock** the door. 记得锁门。／I remember **locking** it. 我记得锁过门。 |
| forget to do / forget doing | 忘记去做／忘记曾做过 | I forgot **to call** him. 我忘了给他打电话。／I'll never forget **meeting** you. 我永远不会忘记与你相遇。 |
| try to do / try doing | 努力设法做／试用某种办法 | I tried **to open** the door, but it was locked. 我试着打开门，但门锁着。／Try **using** another key. 试试用另一把钥匙。 |
| go on doing / go on to do | 继续同一件事／接着做另一件事 | He went on **reading**. 他继续读。／After reading, he went on **to write** a summary. 读完后，他接着写摘要。 |
| need to do / need doing | 主语需要去做／主语需要被处理 | I need **to wash** the car. 我需要洗车。／The car needs **washing**. 这辆车需要清洗。 |
| regret doing / regret to say/tell…（补充） | 后悔做过／遗憾地要说或告知 | I regret **wasting** so much time. 我后悔浪费了这么多时间。／I regret **to say** that I cannot come. 我很遗憾地说，我不能来。 |

**理解顺序：**

- remember to do 中，记得在先、要做的事在后；remember doing 是回忆先前的动作。不能笼统把两种形式等同于“将来时／过去时”。
- try to do 强调努力，但不自动表示成功或失败；try doing 强调把某个动作当作办法来试。
- stop to do 中的 to do 表示停下来之后要做的事，是目的状语；不要认为它与 stop doing 中 doing 的语法作用完全相同。
- need doing 常有被动意义：The car needs washing. = The car needs **to be washed**.

### 11.2 两种形式都常见，不要随意判错

| 动词 | 常见形式 | 初中阶段如何掌握 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| like/love/hate | doing 或 to do | 很多表达喜好、习惯的语境中两者都可；doing 常侧重活动体验，to do 可侧重选择或习惯，不能绝对化 |
| begin/start | doing 或 to do | 常见动作语境中意思接近，如 start raining / start to rain |
| continue（补充） | doing 或 to do | 两种都常用于“继续做”，不要机械套成 go on 两种结构的意思差别 |
| prefer | doing 或 to do | 注意与后面的比较结构配套，见下一小节 |

相关说明参见 [Cambridge：Verb patterns: verb + infinitive or verb + -ing?](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/verb-ing)。

**提醒：**

- **would like/love to do**：表达具体愿望时，先掌握 to do。I'd like **to visit** your school.
- begin/start 后接 understand、realize 等表示认知的动词，通常用 to do：I began **to understand**.
- 前面的 begin/start 已是进行形式时，通常选 to do，避免连续两个 -ing：It is beginning **to rain**.

### 11.3 prefer 的三组表达

| 结构 | 意思 | 自编例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| prefer A to B | 比起 B，更喜欢 A；A、B 可为名词 | I prefer tea **to** coffee. |
| prefer doing A to doing B | 比起做 B，更喜欢做 A；to 是介词 | I prefer **reading** to **watching** TV. |
| prefer to do A rather than do B | 宁愿做 A，而不愿做 B | I prefer **to walk** rather than **take** a bus. |

第三种结构中，rather than 后的不定式常省略 to；带 to 的形式也可能成立，不能把“常省略”讲成“一律禁止”。

### 11.4 补充辨析：afraid、allow、advise

- **be afraid to do**：害怕而不敢做。He is afraid **to speak** in public.
- **be afraid of doing**：害怕做／担心发生某事。He is afraid of **making** mistakes. 两者有语义重叠，结合句意判断。
- **allow sb. to do**：允许某人做；**allow doing**：允许某种行为。They allow students **to use** this room. / They don't allow **smoking** here.
- **advise sb. to do**：建议某人做；**advise doing**：建议做某事。The doctor advised me **to rest**. / The doctor advised **taking** a break.

## 十二、基础补充：doing 与 to do 在句子中的其他常见位置

这部分用于建立知识体系，不是另一张必须死背的固定搭配表。

| 用法 | 自编例句 | 要点 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 动名词短语作主语 | **Reading every day** helps me learn. | 把一个活动作为整体，谓语通常用单数 |
| 不定式作主语，常用 it 占位 | **It** is useful **to read every day**. | it 是形式主语，真正内容在后面 |
| 不定式作表语 | My dream is **to become** a doctor. | 说明梦想、目标等的内容 |
| 动名词作表语 | Her hobby is **collecting** stamps. | 说明爱好是什么，不是“她正在收集”的进行时 |
| 现在分词作定语 | Look at the **sleeping** baby. | sleeping 修饰 baby，表示正在睡觉的 |
| 过去分词作定语 | Don't touch the **broken** glass. | broken 表示已经破碎的状态 |
| 不定式被动形式 | He wants **to be invited**. | 不定式也可表达被动：to be + 过去分词 |

非谓语动词还包括过去分词等内容；本文围绕“后面的动作该用哪一种形式”展开，时态、被动语态和分词的完整体系可另作专题。

## 十三、针对林梓晴的易错对照表

| 常见错写 | 正确写法 | 判断依据 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ask me do | ask me **to do** | ask sb. to do |
| ask me don't do | ask me **not to do** | 否定后面的动作，使用 ask sb. not to do |
| without do | without **doing** | without 是介词 |
| without did | without **doing** | 后面的动作不因过去语境改成过去式 |
| enjoy to read | enjoy **reading** | enjoy 后直接接动作，用 doing |
| finish to do | finish **doing** | finish doing |
| let me to go | let me **go** | 主动句中的 let sb. do |
| make him to wait | make him **wait** | 主动句中的 make sb. do |
| he was made wait | he was made **to wait** | make 的相应被动结构需要 to |
| look forward to see | look forward to **seeing** | 此处 to 是介词 |
| I'm used to get up early（想表达“习惯早起”） | I'm used to **getting** up early | 习惯于做：be used to doing |
| used to getting up（想表达“过去常早起”） | used to **get** up | 过去常常做：used to do |
| suggest him to go | advise him **to go** / suggest **going** | suggest 不套用 advise sb. to do |
| hope him to win | hope **he wins** | hope 后不使用 hope sb. to do |
| had better to go | had better **go** | had better 后用原形 |
| Why not going? | Why not **go**? | Why not do…? |
| How about go? | How about **going**? | about 是介词 |
| spend an hour to read | spend an hour **reading** | spend time doing；也可用 It takes sb. time to do |
| stop to talk（想表达“停止说话”） | stop **talking** | stop to talk 表示停下来去说话，意思不同 |
| pay attention to write | pay attention to **writing** | attention to 中的 to 是介词 |

### 一组句子解决“时态与后接形式”混淆

1. Yesterday, the teacher **asked** me **to read** the text.
2. Yesterday, I **finished** **reading** the text.
3. Yesterday, the teacher **let** me **read** the text.
4. Yesterday, I **left** without **reading** the text.

四句都在说过去的事，但后面的动作分别用 **to read、reading、read、reading**。这由搭配决定；前面的 asked、finished、let、left 承担相应的过去时表达。let 的过去式与原形同形。

## 十四、做题四步法与复习顺序

### 做题四步法

1. **找到空格所在结构。** 不只看紧挨着的一个词，要看前面有没有 ask + sb.、look forward to 等完整搭配。
2. **判断需要的形式。** 是 to do、doing、原形，还是完整从句中的谓语？
3. **核对句意和否定。** 遇到 stop、remember 等尤其要问“去做还是做过”“停止还是转去做”。
4. **检查形式与拼写，再读整句。** 若 to 是不定式标记，后面是否用了原形；若 to 是介词，后面的动作是否按搭配用了 doing？-ing 拼写是否正确？前面谓语的时态是否合适？

### -ing 拼写速查

| 情况 | 示例 |
| --- | --- |
| 通常直接加 -ing | read → reading；play → playing |
| 以不发音 e 结尾，通常去 e 加 -ing | make → making；write → writing |
| 以 ee 结尾，保留 ee | see → seeing；agree → agreeing |
| 重读闭音节且末尾符合双写条件时，双写末尾辅音字母 | run → running；swim → swimming；begin → beginning |
| 以 ie 结尾，变 ie 为 y 再加 -ing | lie → lying；die → dying；tie → tying |

不要把“双写”简化成“辅音字母结尾都双写”：open → opening，visit → visiting。

### 建议分四轮掌握

- **第一轮：修复当前错误。** ask/tell sb. to do；without/by/for doing；let/make sb. do；help sb. (to) do。
- **第二轮：扩大常见搭配。** want/hope/decide/plan to do；enjoy/finish/practise/mind doing；be good at/interested in doing。
- **第三轮：突破易混点。** look forward to；四种 used 结构；stop/remember/forget/try；感官动词 do/doing。
- **第四轮：句型与综合运用。** It takes…、It is…for/of…、too…to…、enough to…、prefer，以及其余补充结构。

每次选 6—8 个结构，用“遮住英文回忆 → 造一个新句 → 隔天混合检查”的方式复习。掌握标准是能独立选形式、解释依据、换句子仍会用。
